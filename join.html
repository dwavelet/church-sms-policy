BibleStudy SMS Enrollment

BibleStudy is a private, conversational SMS group for participating Bible study members. Messages may include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination.

Enrollment in the BibleStudy SMS program is conducted verbally by the BibleStudy group leader. A prospective member's mobile number is not entered into the enrollment system until the member has affirmatively agreed to receive recurring BibleStudy SMS messages.

How Verbal Enrollment Works

  1. The prospective member speaks directly with the BibleStudy group leader.
  2. Before asking for consent or entering the prospective member's mobile number, the group leader reads the complete verbal disclosure shown below.
  3. The disclosure identifies BibleStudy, describes the types of messages that may be sent, states that messages are recurring and that message frequency varies, states that message and data rates may apply, and explains the STOP and HELP commands.
  4. Before asking for consent, the leader verbally directs the prospective member to the exact public website addresses for the BibleStudy Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
  5. The leader then asks whether the prospective member agrees to receive recurring BibleStudy SMS messages.
  6. If the prospective member does not affirmatively agree, the mobile number is not entered into the BibleStudy enrollment system.
  7. If the prospective member affirmatively agrees, the leader enters the mobile number in PENDING status.
  8. BibleStudy sends an SMS confirmation request to the PENDING mobile number.
  9. A PENDING member does not receive ordinary BibleStudy group traffic.
  10. The member must reply YES to the confirmation request before the mobile number becomes ACTIVE.
  11. Only an ACTIVE member may receive ordinary BibleStudy group messages.
  12. A member may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Exact Verbal Consent Script

The BibleStudy group leader reads the following disclosure before obtaining consent and before entering the prospective member's mobile number:

“Would you like to join the BibleStudy SMS group for recurring Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination?

Message frequency varies based on group activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help.

Before you agree, you can review the BibleStudy Privacy Policy at dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html and the Terms and Conditions at dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/terms.html .

If you agree, I will enter your mobile number and BibleStudy will send you a text asking you to confirm. Your number will not become active for regular group messages until you reply YES to that confirmation text.

Do you agree to receive these recurring BibleStudy text messages?”

SMS Confirmation Request

After affirmative verbal agreement, the prospective member's number is entered in PENDING status and BibleStudy sends the following confirmation request:

BibleStudy: You verbally requested to join our recurring SMS group for Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Privacy: https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html Terms: https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/terms.html Reply YES to confirm.

Confirmation and Activation

Replying YES to the confirmation request confirms enrollment. BibleStudy then changes the member's status from PENDING to ACTIVE.

BibleStudy sends the following enrollment confirmation:

BibleStudy: You're confirmed and enrolled in the recurring BibleStudy SMS group. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Message Frequency

Messages are recurring and message frequency varies based on group activity. There is no fixed message schedule.

Message and Data Charges

Msg & data rates may apply depending on the member's mobile carrier and service plan. BibleStudy does not charge members for participation in the SMS group.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to opt out of BibleStudy SMS messages. After an opt-out request is processed, ordinary BibleStudy group messages will no longer be sent to that mobile number unless the member subsequently completes an applicable opt-in process again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact the BibleStudy group leader at mia@dwgifford.com.

Voluntary Participation

Participation in the BibleStudy SMS program is voluntary. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of participating in Bible study activities.
