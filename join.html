BibleStudy SMS Enrollment and Verbal Opt-In

BibleStudy operates a private, conversational SMS group for participating Bible study members. Messages may include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, meeting coordination, and related BibleStudy group communications.

BibleStudy uses verbal opt-in. Prospective members do not enroll by texting a keyword or completing an online form. Consent is obtained personally by the BibleStudy group leader using the procedure and disclosure documented below.

How End Users Consent to Receive Messages

Before obtaining consent or entering a prospective member's mobile number, the BibleStudy group leader reads the exact verbal disclosure shown below.

The disclosure identifies the BibleStudy SMS program, explains the types of messages the member may receive, states that the messages are recurring and that message frequency varies, states that message and data rates may apply, provides STOP and HELP instructions, and directs the prospective member to the exact public URLs for the BibleStudy Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

Only after all of these disclosures have been provided does the leader ask the prospective member to consent to receiving recurring BibleStudy text messages.

Exact Verbal Consent Disclosure

The BibleStudy group leader reads the following disclosure before obtaining consent and before entering the prospective member's mobile number:

“Would you like to join the BibleStudy SMS group for recurring Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination? Message frequency varies based on group activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help.

Before you agree, you can review the BibleStudy Privacy Policy at https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html and the Terms and Conditions at https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/terms.html.

If you agree, I will enter your mobile number and BibleStudy will send you a text asking you to confirm. Your number will not become active for regular group messages until you reply YES to that confirmation text.

Do you agree to receive these recurring BibleStudy text messages?”

Public Privacy Policy and Terms

Before consent is requested, the prospective member is verbally directed to the following public documents:

  • Privacy Policy:
    https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html
  • Terms and Conditions:
    https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/terms.html

What Happens After Verbal Consent

  1. The prospective member receives the complete verbal disclosure, including the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions URLs.
  2. The prospective member is asked whether they agree to receive recurring BibleStudy text messages.
  3. If the prospective member does not affirmatively agree, the mobile number is not entered into the BibleStudy enrollment system.
  4. If the prospective member affirmatively agrees, the group leader enters the mobile number into the BibleStudy system in PENDING status.
  5. BibleStudy sends the SMS confirmation request shown below to the PENDING mobile number.
  6. A PENDING member does not receive ordinary BibleStudy group messages.
  7. The member must reply YES to the confirmation request.
  8. After YES is received, the member becomes ACTIVE and may receive ordinary BibleStudy group messages.

YES does not initiate enrollment. BibleStudy obtains verbal consent first. YES confirms the previously provided verbal consent and is required before the member is activated for regular group messaging.

Opt-In Confirmation Message

After the prospective member has affirmatively consented verbally and the number has been placed in PENDING status, BibleStudy sends the following SMS:

BibleStudy: You verbally agreed to receive recurring BibleStudy texts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply YES to confirm, HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Privacy: https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html Terms: https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/terms.html

Confirmation and Activation

When the member replies YES, BibleStudy confirms the previously provided verbal consent and changes the member's status from PENDING to ACTIVE.

BibleStudy then sends:

BibleStudy: You're confirmed and enrolled in the recurring BibleStudy SMS group. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Opt-In Keywords

BibleStudy does not use an SMS keyword to initiate enrollment. Enrollment is initiated only after affirmative verbal consent is obtained by the BibleStudy group leader.

YES is used only to confirm previously obtained verbal consent before activating the member for ordinary BibleStudy group messaging. YES does not initiate the opt-in process.

Message Frequency

BibleStudy messages are recurring. Message frequency varies based on group activity. There is no fixed message schedule.

Message and Data Rates

Msg & data rates may apply depending on the member's mobile carrier and service plan. BibleStudy does not charge members for participation in the SMS group.

Opt-Out

Members may reply STOP at any time to opt out of BibleStudy SMS messages. After an opt-out request is processed, ordinary BibleStudy group messages will no longer be sent to that mobile number unless the member subsequently completes an applicable opt-in process again.

Help

Members may reply HELP for assistance or contact the BibleStudy group leader at mia@dwgifford.com.

Voluntary Participation

Participation in the BibleStudy SMS program is voluntary. Consent to receive BibleStudy SMS messages is not a condition of participating in Bible study meetings, activities, or other BibleStudy activities.
