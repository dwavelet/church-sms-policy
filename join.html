BibleStudy SMS Enrollment

BibleStudy is a private, conversational SMS group for Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination. Enrollment is handled personally by the group leader using the verbal consent procedure below.

How enrollment works

  1. The group leader reads the verbal SMS consent disclosure below.
  2. The prospective member must affirmatively agree before the mobile number is entered.
  3. The number is entered only in pending status.
  4. BibleStudy sends an SMS asking the member to reply YES to confirm.
  5. The number becomes active for group messaging only after the member replies YES.
  6. The member may reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help.

Verbal consent script

“Would you like to join the BibleStudy SMS group for recurring Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination? Message frequency varies based on group activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help. If you agree, I will enter your mobile number and BibleStudy will send you a text asking you to confirm. Do you agree to receive these messages?”

Only an affirmative response authorizes the leader to enter the number into the pending enrollment process. The member must still reply YES to the subsequent confirmation request before ordinary group messages begin.
