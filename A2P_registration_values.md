# BibleStudy A2P 10DLC — Verbal Opt-In Registration Values

## Opt-in type
Verbal only.

## Campaign description
BibleStudy operates a Twilio-hosted SMS relay application for two-way group communication among opted-in members of a small Bible study group. Prospective members are enrolled personally by the group leader. Before a mobile number is entered, the leader reads the documented verbal SMS disclosure describing the recurring BibleStudy SMS program, message types, variable message frequency, possible message and data rates, and STOP and HELP instructions. Only after the prospective member affirmatively agrees is the number entered in pending status. BibleStudy then sends an SMS requesting final confirmation. The number becomes active for group messaging only after the member replies YES. Messages include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination. Members may reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help.

## How do end-users consent to receive messages?
End users opt in verbally through the BibleStudy group leader. Before entering a prospective member's mobile number, the leader reads this disclosure: “Would you like to join the BibleStudy SMS group for recurring Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination? Message frequency varies based on group activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help. If you agree, I will enter your mobile number and BibleStudy will send you a text asking you to confirm. Do you agree to receive these messages?” Only users who affirmatively agree are entered into the system, initially in pending status. BibleStudy then sends an SMS confirmation request. The user must reply YES before the number becomes active and receives ordinary group messages. Users may reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help. ## Required application state
PENDING: verbally consented but not yet confirmed by SMS; receives only enrollment/administrative messages.
ACTIVE: replied YES; may receive and send normal group traffic.
OPTED_OUT: STOP received; ordinary and enrollment traffic suppressed except permitted opt-out handling.
