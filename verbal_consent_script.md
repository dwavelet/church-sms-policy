# BibleStudy Verbal SMS Consent Script

Read this disclosure before entering a prospective member's mobile number:

> Would you like to join the BibleStudy SMS group for recurring Bible study discussion,
> prayer requests, and meeting coordination? Message frequency varies based on group
> activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out
> or HELP for help. If you agree, I will enter your mobile number and BibleStudy will
> send you a text asking you to confirm. Do you agree to receive these messages?

## Procedure

1. Do not enter the number unless the prospective member affirmatively agrees.
2. Record the number as PENDING, not ACTIVE.
3. Send the SMS confirmation request.
4. Do not relay ordinary group traffic to the number while PENDING.
5. Activate the number only after receiving YES.
6. Send the enrollment confirmation.
7. STOP must suppress future group traffic and record the opt-out.
8. HELP must return BibleStudy identification and assistance information.
