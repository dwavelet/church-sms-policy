Privacy Policy

BibleStudy
Effective date: August 27, 2026

Overview

BibleStudy operates a private, conversational SMS group for participating Bible study members. Messages may include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BibleStudy collects in connection with the SMS program, how that information is used, and how mobile information and messaging consent are protected.

Information We Collect

BibleStudy may collect and maintain information necessary to operate the SMS group, including:

  • A participating member's name;
  • Mobile telephone number;
  • Date and time of verbal SMS consent;
  • Identity of the person who recorded the verbal consent;
  • Date and time an SMS confirmation request was sent;
  • Date and time of the member's YES confirmation;
  • Current enrollment status;
  • Opt-out information, including STOP requests; and
  • SMS messages sent to or through the BibleStudy group.

How We Use Information

BibleStudy uses this information only as reasonably necessary to operate and administer the BibleStudy SMS program. This includes:

  • Managing enrollment in the SMS group;
  • Documenting and confirming messaging consent;
  • Sending and receiving BibleStudy group messages;
  • Processing HELP and STOP requests;
  • Maintaining opt-in and opt-out records;
  • Providing Bible study discussion and prayer-request communications;
  • Coordinating BibleStudy meetings and related activities; and
  • Maintaining the technical operation and integrity of the SMS service.

Mobile Information and Messaging Consent

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile opt-in information and consent are used only for the BibleStudy SMS program and for purposes reasonably necessary to operate, administer, and support that program.

SMS consent is specific to BibleStudy. Consent to receive BibleStudy SMS messages is not transferred to another organization for that organization's marketing or promotional use.

SMS Enrollment and Consent

BibleStudy uses a verbal enrollment process.

Before a prospective member is asked to consent, the BibleStudy group leader explains the recurring SMS program, the types of messages, variable message frequency, possible message and data charges, STOP and HELP instructions, and directs the prospective member to this Privacy Policy and the BibleStudy Terms and Conditions.

The prospective member must affirmatively agree before the mobile number is entered into the BibleStudy system.

After verbal agreement, the number is placed in PENDING status and BibleStudy sends an SMS confirmation request. The member must reply YES before the number becomes ACTIVE for ordinary BibleStudy group messaging.

The complete enrollment procedure and verbal consent disclosure are available on the BibleStudy SMS Enrollment page.

Message Frequency

BibleStudy messages are recurring. Message frequency varies based on group activity. There is no fixed message schedule.

Message and Data Charges

Msg & data rates may apply depending on the member's mobile carrier and service plan. BibleStudy does not charge members for participation in the SMS group.

Opting Out

Members may reply STOP to opt out of BibleStudy SMS messages.

After an opt-out request is processed, ordinary BibleStudy group messages will no longer be sent to that mobile number unless the member subsequently completes an applicable opt-in process again.

Help

Members may reply HELP for assistance or contact the BibleStudy group leader at mia@dwgifford.com.

Service Providers

BibleStudy may use service providers as necessary to operate the SMS service, including telecommunications, messaging, hosting, and technical service providers.

Information provided to such service providers is used as necessary to provide or support the BibleStudy SMS service and is not provided by BibleStudy to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Information Security

BibleStudy takes reasonable measures to protect information used to administer the SMS program from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes affecting the BibleStudy SMS program or its consent practices will be reflected in this policy.
