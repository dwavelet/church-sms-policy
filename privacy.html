Privacy Policy

BibleStudy
Effective date: 08-24-2026

What we collect

We collect a mobile phone number when a prospective member voluntarily provides it to the BibleStudy group leader after affirmatively agreeing to the verbal SMS consent disclosure. We may also retain records associated with enrollment, confirmation, opt-out, and help requests.

How we use it

The number is initially used to send an SMS confirmation request. The number is not activated for ordinary BibleStudy group messaging until the member replies YES to confirm. Once confirmed, the number is used to operate the BibleStudy SMS group, including delivery of authorized group messages and administration of HELP and STOP requests.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share mobile opt-in data, consent, or phone numbers with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be disclosed to service providers only as necessary to operate and support the SMS service, or when required by law. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS program disclosures

BibleStudy messages are recurring and conversational. Message frequency varies based on group activity. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Opt-out

Members may opt out at any time by replying STOP. An opt-out confirmation will be sent and no further BibleStudy group messages will be sent unless the member subsequently opts in again.

Data retention

Active roster information is retained only as necessary to operate the group. Enrollment and opt-out records may be retained as reasonably necessary for compliance, security, and legal purposes.
