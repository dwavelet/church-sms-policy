Privacy Policy

BibleStudy
Effective date: 08-22-2026

What we collect

We collect mobile phone numbers provided voluntarily by members who choose to participate in the Bible Study SMS group.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver group text messages among registered Bible Study members via a shared group SMS number.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share mobile opt-in data or phone numbers with any third party for any purpose not described in this policy. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-out

Members may opt out of the SMS group at any time by replying STOP to any group message or by contacting the group leader directly. Upon opting out, your number will be removed from the roster within one business day.

Data retention

Phone numbers are retained only as long as a member participates in the group. Numbers are deleted from the roster upon request or when a member leaves the group.
