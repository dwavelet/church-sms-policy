BibleStudy SMS Program
Effective date: August 27, 2026

Program Description

BibleStudy operates a private, conversational SMS group for participating Bible study members. Messages may include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, meeting coordination, and related BibleStudy group communications.

The BibleStudy SMS program is a recurring messaging program. Message frequency varies based on group activity.

Participation and Consent

Enrollment in the BibleStudy SMS program is conducted verbally through the BibleStudy group leader.

Before asking a prospective member to consent and before entering the prospective member's mobile number, the group leader explains that:

  • BibleStudy sends recurring SMS messages;
  • Messages may include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, meeting coordination, and related group communications;
  • Message frequency varies based on group activity;
  • Message and data rates may apply;
  • The member may reply STOP at any time to opt out; and
  • The member may reply HELP for assistance.

Before asking for consent, the group leader also verbally directs the prospective member to the BibleStudy Privacy Policy at:

https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html

and the BibleStudy Terms and Conditions at:

https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/terms.html

Only after these disclosures are provided does the group leader ask whether the prospective member agrees to receive recurring BibleStudy SMS messages.

A prospective member must affirmatively agree before the mobile number is entered into the BibleStudy enrollment system.

Verbal Consent and SMS Confirmation

An affirmative verbal response begins the enrollment process but does not immediately activate the member for ordinary BibleStudy group messaging.

After affirmative verbal consent, the group leader enters the prospective member's mobile number in PENDING status. BibleStudy then sends an SMS confirmation request to that mobile number.

A prospective member must reply YES to the SMS confirmation request before the mobile number becomes ACTIVE and begins receiving ordinary BibleStudy group messages.

A mobile number that remains in PENDING status does not receive ordinary BibleStudy group traffic.

The complete verbal enrollment procedure and consent disclosure are publicly available on the BibleStudy SMS Enrollment page.

Message Frequency

BibleStudy SMS messages are recurring. Message frequency varies based on group activity. There is no fixed message schedule.

Message and Data Rates

Msg & data rates may apply depending on the member's mobile carrier and service plan.

BibleStudy does not charge members for participation in the SMS group. Members are responsible for any messaging, data, or other charges imposed by their mobile carrier or service provider.

Opt-Out

Members may reply STOP at any time to opt out of BibleStudy SMS messages.

After an opt-out request is processed, ordinary BibleStudy group messages will no longer be sent to that mobile number unless the member subsequently completes an applicable opt-in process again.

Phone Number Changes

If a member's mobile phone number changes, that member is not liable or responsible for BibleStudy SMS messages sent to the previously assigned number prior to notification of the change.

Members whose phone number changes should notify the BibleStudy group leader at mia@dwgifford.com so the roster can be updated. BibleStudy assumes responsibility for removing or updating stale numbers from the enrollment system promptly upon notification.

BibleStudy will not hold a member responsible for opting out of messages sent to a number the member no longer controls.

Help

Members may reply HELP for assistance or contact the BibleStudy group leader at mia@dwgifford.com.

Voluntary Participation

Participation in the BibleStudy SMS program is voluntary.

Consent to receive BibleStudy SMS messages is not a condition of participating in Bible study meetings, activities, or other BibleStudy activities.

Member Messages

BibleStudy is a conversational group messaging service. Participating members may send messages intended for other participating members of the BibleStudy group.

Members should not send unlawful, threatening, abusive, or inappropriate content through the BibleStudy SMS program.

Privacy

Information collected and used in connection with the BibleStudy SMS program is handled according to the BibleStudy Privacy Policy.

The Privacy Policy is available at:

https://dwavelet.github.io/church-sms-policy/privacy.html

BibleStudy does not share, sell, rent, or provide a member's mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Supported Carriers and Delivery

SMS delivery depends on participating mobile carriers, networks, devices, and service availability. Delivery of every message cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to the SMS Program

BibleStudy may modify or discontinue the SMS program. Material changes affecting messaging practices, enrollment, or consent requirements will be reflected in the applicable BibleStudy program documentation.

These Terms and Conditions may be updated from time to time. The current version will be made available on this page.

Contact

Questions about these Terms and Conditions or the BibleStudy SMS program may be directed to the BibleStudy group leader at mia@dwgifford.com.
