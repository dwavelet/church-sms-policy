Program description

This is a private, conversational SMS group for members of the BibleStudy group. Messages are sent and received by participating members through a shared group phone number.

Participation

Participation is voluntary and by invitation of the group leader. Members consent to receive SMS messages from other group members by providing their phone number to the group leader.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on group activity. There is no fixed schedule — messages are conversational and sent as needed by group members.

Costs

Msg & data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. BibleStudy does not charge members for participation.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any message to be removed from the group. You will receive one confirmation message and no further group messages will be sent to your number.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact the group leader directly at mia@dwgifford.com

Conduct

Members agree to communicate respectfully and in keeping with the values of BibleStudy. The group leader reserves the right to remove any member at any time.

Changes

These terms may be updated at any time. Continued participation in the group constitutes acceptance of any updated terms.
