BibleStudy

Program description

BibleStudy is a private, conversational SMS group for participating Bible study members. Messages may include Bible study discussion, prayer requests, and meeting coordination and are exchanged through a shared group SMS number.

Participation and consent

Participation in the SMS program is voluntary. Before a mobile number is entered, the BibleStudy group leader verbally explains the program, including the types of messages, recurring nature of the program, variable message frequency, possible message and data charges, and STOP and HELP instructions. The prospective member must affirmatively agree before the number is entered.

After verbal agreement, the number is placed in pending status and BibleStudy sends an SMS confirmation request. The member must reply YES before the number becomes active for ordinary group messaging. Consent to SMS messaging is not a condition of participating in Bible study activities.

Message frequency

Messages are recurring and message frequency varies based on group activity. There is no fixed schedule.

Costs

Msg & data rates may apply depending on the member's mobile carrier plan. BibleStudy does not charge members for participation in the SMS group.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to opt out. An opt-out confirmation will be sent, and no further BibleStudy group messages will be sent unless the member subsequently opts in again.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact the group leader at mia@dwgifford.com.

Privacy

Mobile opt-in data and consent are handled as described in the BibleStudy Privacy Policy.

Conduct

Members agree to communicate respectfully and in keeping with the values of BibleStudy. The group leader may remove a member from the group.
